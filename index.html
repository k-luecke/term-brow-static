Kyle Luecke

Personal reading space for independent research, public-record investigations, and verification systems.

Kyle Luecke works in outside-plant fiber infrastructure and spends much of his time outside of it investigating technical problems. He founded Driftbound Labs as a home for reproducible experiments in verification, measurement, and dependable systems. termbrow.org is his personal index for case studies, engineering notes, and bounded claims.

Projects here are presented as question → method → evidence → limits → source. GitHub and Driftbound Labs Papers remain the source of truth for code, hashes, and test suites. Start with Paxiom, IntentNativeOS, Time Integrity, or 811Dog — or read Method first.
