Phase Selects; It Does Not Amplify

Twelve preregistered stages (12/12 Bitcoin OpenTimestamps proofs) testing what oscillatory phase gating actually buys in Mixture-of-Experts routing — and what fails under null and lookup controls.

v1.0.0 PREPRINT12/12 BITCOIN OTSAMPLIFICATION NULL

Question

Does oscillatory phase modulation in expert routing amplify ensemble representational capacity, or does it act strictly as a temporal selection, persistence, and attribution channel?

Supported mechanisms

Across twelve Bitcoin-anchored preregistered stages, tonic phase gating establishes multi-token expert hold persistence (T_hold = 26.8 and 65.0 tokens), clean orthogonal cosine demodulation of multiplexed expert streams, and single-pass phase-tagged expert attribution (r_φ = 0.869).

Negative & closed gates

Oscillatory gating produces null ensemble quality amplification over matched static routers; direct lookup tables dominate continuous evidence routing when clean regime labels exist; and stage T10 is explicitly closed as not informatively tested rather than massaged.

Takeaway

Phase is a control and observability coordinate (selection persistence + single-pass attribution), not a free accuracy multiplier.
