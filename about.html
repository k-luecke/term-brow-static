Bio

I build systems that have to stay honest when the source moves, the channel is noisy, or the incentive is to overclaim. The public work here is independent and self-directed: public-record pipelines, cryptographic primitives, and preregistered measurement packages. TermBrow is the reading layer; GitHub holds implementation, tests, and provenance.

By day I am an Assistant Project Manager at Ervin Cable Construction on FTTP deployment — coordinating aerial and underground fiber work across crews, engineering, utilities, permits, and closeout. I was promoted from Crew Supervisor to Area Supervisor to APM. The job keeps the same constraint as the research: plans and reality diverge, and you only get credit for what you can show.

Before that I worked as a glazier and an electrical apprentice. I hold a B.S. in Psychological Sciences from Western Kentucky University (2018), finished in three years, with quantitative methods, experimental design, statistics, and cognition / biobehavioral dynamics. That training shows up less as psychology content than as habit: declare the decision rule before you look, keep negative results visible, and do not widen a claim past what the artifact supports.

The compact professional record is on the CV. How an uncertain question becomes a checkable claim is on the method page. Selected projects start on the index.
