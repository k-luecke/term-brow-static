Bio

Kyle Luecke works in infrastructure and spends much of his time outside of it investigating technical problems. He founded Drift Bound Labs as a home for independent experiments in verification, measurement, and dependable systems. termbrow.org is his personal space for the work, notes, and questions that don't fit neatly anywhere else.

Day job: Assistant Project Manager at Ervin Cable Construction on FTTP deployment — coordinating aerial and underground fiber across crews, engineering, utilities, permits, and closeout. Promoted from Crew Supervisor to Area Supervisor to APM. Earlier work was in the trades (glazing, then electrical apprenticeship). B.S. Psychological Sciences, Western Kentucky University, 2018.

The compact professional record is on the CV. How an uncertain question becomes a checkable claim is on the method page. Selected projects start on the index. Drift Bound Labs lives at driftboundlabs.com and github.com/k-luecke/driftbound-labs.
