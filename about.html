Operating Structure & Scope

Kyle Luecke works in outside-plant fiber construction management and founded Driftbound Labs LLC as the operating company and venture vehicle for his commercial software platforms, physical-layer infrastructure research, and scoped technical diligence engagements — including Paxiom (cryptographic execution and payment-delivery receipts at paxiom.org). For institutional research, data licensing, or verification sprints, see driftboundlabs.com/ventures.html.

termbrow.org is his personal engineering notebook, rendered inside the web port of his open-source TermBrow terminal browser: a place for long-form technical notes, 20 project case studies, and negative experimental gates where failed hypotheses remain part of the permanent public record.

Day job: Assistant Project Manager in outside-plant FTTP deployment — coordinating aerial and underground fiber across crews, engineering, utilities, permits, and closeout (promoted from Crew Supervisor → Area Supervisor → APM). Earlier work was in the trades (commercial glazing, then electrical apprenticeship). B.S. Psychological Sciences, Western Kentucky University, 2018.

Formal preprints and whitepapers with SHA-256 manifests and Bitcoin OpenTimestamps proofs are archived at driftboundlabs.com/papers.html (ORCID 0009-0008-0202-7852).
