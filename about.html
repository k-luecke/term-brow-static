Bio

Kyle Luecke works in outside-plant fiber infrastructure and spends much of his time outside of it investigating technical problems. He founded Driftbound Labs as a home for independent experiments in verification, measurement, and dependable systems, and ships open execution-provenance tooling at paxiom.org. termbrow.org is his personal space for case studies, engineering notes, and questions that span multiple domains.

Day job: Assistant Project Manager in outside-plant FTTP deployment — coordinating aerial and underground fiber across crews, engineering, utilities, permits, and closeout. Promoted from Crew Supervisor to Area Supervisor to APM. Earlier work was in the trades (glazing, then electrical apprenticeship). B.S. Psychological Sciences, Western Kentucky University, 2018.

The compact professional record is on the CV. How an uncertain question becomes a checkable claim is on the method page. Published preprints and whitepapers live at driftboundlabs.com/papers.html (ORCID 0009-0008-0202-7852).
